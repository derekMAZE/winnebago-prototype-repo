How to click through this prototype

  1. Start on Home. From there, use the real navigation — Shop by Category or Find Your Fit — to move through the site, just as a shopper would.
  2. Every page is fully linked: Home, Shop by Category, Find Your Fit, and a Product Detail page all connect to one another through their actual buttons, cards, and nav links.
  3. Clicking Add Vehicle (or Find Your Fit) opens the vehicle quiz. Completing it — or already having a vehicle selected — is what determines whether a part shows as compatible, needs verification, or doesn't fit.
  4. There's no fixed order to follow. Click around like a real shopper would, and use your browser's back button to return to a previous screen.
  5. The desktop flow below is the same experience as the mobile flow — just built for a 390px screen.

Note: this is a click-through concept for RFP evaluation, not a live storefront. Some buttons and links are placeholders.
