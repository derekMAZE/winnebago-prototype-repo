How to click through this prototype

Click straight through every page below. Each button, card, and nav link is fully wired, just like a real shopper would use it.

Home
Functionality
  • Search for parts and browse categories
  • Start Find Your Fit to identify your RV
  • Open Ask About Parts to chat
→
Shop by Category
Functionality
  • Browse the full category grid
  • Filter by brand, price, and rating
  • See a fitment badge on every product
→
Product Detail
Functionality
  • Check compatibility for your RV
  • Add the part to your cart
  • Ask about this part in chat
Find Your Fit
Functionality
  • Answer the vehicle quiz
  • Save a vehicle profile
  • Unlock Fits, Verify First, and Doesn't Fit badges everywhere
  • There's no fixed order. Click around like a real shopper, and use your browser's back button to return to a previous screen.
  • The desktop flow below is the same experience as the mobile flow, just built for a 390px screen.

Note: this is a click-through concept for RFP evaluation, not a live storefront. Some buttons and links are placeholders.
