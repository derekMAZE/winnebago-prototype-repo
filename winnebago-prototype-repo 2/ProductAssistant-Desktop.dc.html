How hard is it to install?
Parts Assistant
Straightforward. Most owners swap this pump in 20–30 minutes: disconnect the two water lines and the 12V leads at the old pump, unbolt it from the mounting bracket, and reverse the steps with the new one. No cutting or rewiring.
Source: Install guide WGO-IG-000164 rev C, p. 2

What tools do I need?
Parts Assistant
A Phillips screwdriver, an adjustable wrench for the water line fittings, and a pair of pliers for the hose clamps. No specialty tools.
